You've probably seen it. The post you smoothed out with AI gets a few likes from people you already know. The one you wrote quickly, about something that actually happened, gets people replying with their own version. Noggin is built around that second kind of post.

How it works

From a chat to a post you'd be proud of

01

Talk it through

A deep dive interview, twelve questions over three short sittings. Type, or send voice notes if that's easier.

02

Your noggin fills up

Every answer is sorted into your whys, stories, opinions, personality and receipts, and it keeps growing as you add more.

03

Studio picks what's worth saying

Each week it suggests what to post and why, then drafts it from your own words and phrases.

04

You post it

Edit, approve, publish. Nothing goes out without you. Scheduling straight to LinkedIn is on the way.

Questions people ask

Will it actually sound like me?+

It drafts from what you've told it, in the phrases you use. You'll still want to edit, and the more you add, the closer it gets.

Does it post for me?+

Not on its own. You approve every post. Scheduling straight to LinkedIn is coming, and even then nothing goes out without your say.

Is it safe for my LinkedIn account?+

Noggin will only ever connect through LinkedIn's official API. No cookies, no browser extensions.

How is this different from other AI writing tools?+

Most start from a prompt. Noggin starts from an interview and a memory of everything you've said, so every draft has something real to work with.
